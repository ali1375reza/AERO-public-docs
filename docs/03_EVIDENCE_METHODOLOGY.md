# Evidence methodology

## Evidence classes

| Class | What it can show | What it cannot show |
|---|---|---|
| Modeled / paper | Whether assumptions produce a coherent economic estimate | Real capture, competition, or realized P&L |
| Historical replay | Whether a past state and path can be reproduced | That the same opportunity will appear again |
| Fork stress | Whether controlled mechanics can complete under a chosen state | Live timing, competition, or real profit |
| Real-state shadow | Whether current data, freshness, and safety checks behave correctly | A signed/broadcast transaction or realized income |
| Realized | Actual post-cost result from controlled live operation | A guarantee of future performance |

## Evidence record requirements

Every public result should state:

- the observation date and time window;
- the evidence class;
- the scope of markets/positions in coarse public language;
- whether an opportunity was observed or `NOT_OBSERVED`;
- the outcome and the main limitation;
- a pointer to the private evidence package or public report hash, when appropriate.

## Status vocabulary

- **PROVEN:** the stated gate passed within its declared class.
- **SUPPORTED:** independent observations support the claim, but a stronger gate remains.
- **CONDITIONAL:** the result depends on an assumption or a later gate.
- **NOT_OBSERVED:** the required event did not occur in the window.
- **NOT_PROVEN:** the evidence required for the claim does not yet exist.
- **BLOCKED:** a technical or operational dependency prevents the next gate.

## Integrity and chronology

The public record keeps failed versions and negative results. When a report is frozen, its publication date and, where safe, a digest of the public report are recorded. A later correction is additive and does not erase the prior state.

## Profit language

Modeled surplus, stress profit, historical surplus, and shadow approval are not realized profit. Commercial claims require realized net results after all relevant costs and a separate controlled review.
