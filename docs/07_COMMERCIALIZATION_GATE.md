# Commercialization gate

AERO is a proprietary platform intended for controlled licensing or investor-facing monitoring in the future. It is not a public retail bot and this repository is not an offer of financial service.

## Required before a commercial claim

1. repeated real-trigger evidence;
2. realized net results after gas, L1/L2 costs, swap costs, slippage, failures, and operational overhead;
3. reproducible monitoring and retention;
4. controlled custody, caps, kill switch, and manual escalation;
5. legal/IP and third-party dependency review;
6. evidence that a customer or license market exists.

The project’s internal policy uses three consecutive positive months of realized net results as a minimum commercial gate. A zero or negative month resets the streak. This is deliberately stricter than a backtest, a stress run, or a single successful transaction.

## What the public repository can support

It can support credibility around process, engineering discipline, evidence boundaries, failure handling, and roadmap maturity. It cannot support a guarantee of income or a claim that the private engine is ready for unrestricted deployment.
